How to Play Texas Hold'em

Goal: Make the best 5-card hand using your 2 hole cards and 5 community cards. Win chips by having the best hand — or by making everyone else fold.

Hand Rankings (High to Low)

  1. Royal Flush — A, K, Q, J, 10 of same suit
  2. Straight Flush — 5 in a row of same suit
  3. Four of a Kind — 4 cards of same rank
  4. Full House — 3 of a kind + pair
  5. Flush — 5 of same suit
  6. Straight — 5 in a row
  7. Three of a Kind — 3 cards of same rank
  8. Two Pair — 2 different pairs
  9. One Pair — 2 cards of same rank
  10. High Card — Highest single card

Betting Rounds

There are 4 rounds of betting per hand:

  • Pre-flop — after hole cards are dealt
  • Flop — after 3 community cards
  • Turn — after 4th community card
  • River — after 5th community card

Actions

  • Fold — give up your hand
  • Check — pass (only if no bet to call)
  • Call — match the current bet
  • Raise — bet more than the current bet
  • All In — bet all your chips

Blinds

Each hand starts with two forced bets — the small blind and big blind — posted by the two players left of the dealer. This ensures there's always something to play for.
